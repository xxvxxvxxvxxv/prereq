## What is not inferred

A course listed as an option is not necessarily mandatory, offered this semester, available to the selected student, or free of consent/minimum-grade requirements. The same course may qualify in multiple areas even when the official pool page lists it only in the highest-priority area. Graduate exceptions may not appear in undergraduate lists.

The data stores course codes, titles, SU/ECTS credits, faculty labels and official URLs, not full copyrighted course descriptions. Known bundled faculty-course flags are retained; unverified flags are null rather than falsely marked negative. Program rules are paraphrased in the seed and linked to their authority. Live adapters retain the source rule wording.

Live snapshots include retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 hashes of the decoded pages observed by the fetcher. A hash identifies a response; it is not a university signature or a proof of correctness.

Admission-term degree requirements and current prerequisite details are intentionally separate. The course catalog may have changed since a student entered the university.
